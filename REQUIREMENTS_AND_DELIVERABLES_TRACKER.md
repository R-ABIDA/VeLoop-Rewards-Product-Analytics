## 1. Evidentiary Classification Framework

In accordance with strict project integrity directives, all requirements, specifications, and data artifacts are categorized into one of four mutually exclusive evidentiary tiers:

1. **[Confirmed]**: Information verified directly from official source documents or verified system parameters.
2. **[Proposed]**: Professional analytical recommendations, architectural designs, schemas, or metrics formulated by the Data Analyst.
3. **[To Be Verified]**: Assumptions, business thresholds, conversion rules, or missing parameters that require explicit sign-off from VELOOP leadership or engineering.
4. **[Simulated]**: Data, behavioral distributions, event logs, and testing outputs generated or supplied strictly for analytical testing. Never cited as actual company performance.
