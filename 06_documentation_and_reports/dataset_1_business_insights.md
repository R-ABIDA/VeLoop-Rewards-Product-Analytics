# VELoop Rewards – Dataset 1 Business Insights and Recommendations

**Project:** VELoop Rewards – Product Analytics Dashboard  
**Dataset:** `VELoop_Rewards_Data_01.csv`  
**Database Table:** `veloop.users`  
**Records Analyzed:** 1,000  
**Domain:** Data Analysis

---

## 1. Overview

This report presents business insights from the analysis of Dataset 1, containing 1,000 user records. The analysis focuses on account status, onboarding, acquisition channels, geographical distribution, demographics, devices, verification, consent, XP balances, user activity, and registration trends.

The purpose is to understand the available user profile data and identify opportunities to improve user engagement, onboarding, and product adoption.

## 2. Key Business Insights

### 2.1 Account Status

- 568 users (56.8%) have active accounts.
- 283 users (28.3%) are inactive.
- 77 users (7.7%) are suspended.
- 72 users (7.2%) are pending verification.

**Business Insight:** A considerable proportion of registered users are inactive. Improving user engagement and reviewing inactive accounts may help increase platform activity.

**Recommendation:** Introduce targeted re-engagement campaigns and investigate reasons for account inactivity.

### 2.2 Onboarding Performance

- 722 users (72.2%) completed onboarding.
- 146 users (14.6%) started onboarding.
- 132 users (13.2%) have not started onboarding.

**Business Insight:** Most users completed onboarding, but 278 users have not completed the process.

**Recommendation:** Simplify onboarding steps and provide reminders or guidance to users who have not completed registration.

### 2.3 User Acquisition

- Organic Search: 244 users (24.4%).
- Direct: 217 users (21.7%).
- Social Media: 160 users (16%).
- Referral: 119 users (11.9%).
- WhatsApp: 103 users (10.3%).
- Other: 102 users (10.2%).
- Telegram: 55 users (5.5%).

**Business Insight:** Organic Search is the largest recorded acquisition source, followed by Direct traffic.

**Recommendation:** Continue monitoring organic search performance and evaluate the effectiveness of social media and referral campaigns.

### 2.4 Geographical Distribution

- Kerala has the highest number of users with 129.
- Uttar Pradesh has 122 users.
- Bihar has 92 users.
- Kochi is the leading city with 129 users.
- Bhubaneswar has 83 users.

**Business Insight:** User registrations are concentrated in some states and cities.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate regional engagement and consider location-specific awareness campaigns.

### 2.5 Age Group Distribution

- 18–24 years: 354 users (35.4%).
- 25–34 years: 448 users (44.8%).
- 35–44 years: 198 users (19.8%).

**Business Insight:** Users aged 25–34 form the largest age group, followed by users aged 18–24.

**Recommendation:** Design relevant engagement campaigns and reward experiences for the major age groups.

### 2.6 Gender Distribution

- Male: 343 users (34.3%).
- Female: 331 users (33.1%).
- Prefer not to say: 326 users (32.6%).

**Business Insight:** The recorded gender categories have relatively balanced user counts.

**Recommendation:** Keep engagement strategies inclusive and respect users' privacy preferences.

### 2.7 Platform and Device Usage

- Web platform: 1,000 users (100%).
- Android: 659 users (65.9%).
- Windows: 155 users (15.5%).
- iOS: 140 users (14%).
- macOS: 46 users (4.6%).

**Business Insight:** All records indicate Web as the platform, while Android is the most common primary device.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize a smooth web experience across devices, especially Android devices.

### 2.8 Email Verification

- Verified: 855 users (85.5%).
- Pending: 145 users (14.5%).

**Business Insight:** Most users have verified their email addresses, but some accounts still require verification.

**Recommendation:** Provide verification reminders and make the verification process easy to complete.

### 2.9 Consent Status

- Accepted: 923 users (92.3%).
- Pending: 77 users (7.7%).

**Business Insight:** Most users have accepted the recorded consent status.

**Recommendation:** Maintain transparent consent practices and ensure users can manage their preferences.

### 2.10 Marketing Opt-in

- Opted in: 442 users (44.2%).
- Not opted in: 558 users (55.8%).

**Business Insight:** More than half of the users have not opted into marketing communication.

**Recommendation:** Use relevant, permission-based communication and explain the benefits of optional marketing updates.

### 2.11 XP Balance

- Average XP: 231.89.
- Minimum XP: 5.
- Maximum XP: 1,156.
- Total XP: 231,889.

**Business Insight:** Users have different XP balances, indicating variation in recorded reward points.

**Recommendation:** Review XP distribution and consider reward milestones to encourage participation. Actual earning and redemption patterns cannot be established from this profile table alone.

### 2.12 User Activity

- Active in the last 7 days: 78 users (7.8%).
- Inactive in the last 7 days: 922 users (92.2%).
- Active in the last 30 days: 325 users (32.5%).
- Inactive in the last 30 days: 675 users (67.5%).

**Business Insight:** The recorded activity flags show a large inactive user group.

**Recommendation:** Investigate inactivity and consider appropriate re-engagement campaigns. These figures represent the supplied activity flags, not independently measured session events.

### 2.13 Registration Trend

| Month | Registered Users |
|---|---:|
| April 2026 | 174 |
| May 2026 | 179 |
| June 2026 | 166 |
| July 2026 | 198 |
| August 2026 | 192 |
| September 2026 | 91 |

**Business Insight:** July recorded the highest number of registrations. September has fewer registrations in the supplied data.

**Recommendation:** Monitor registration trends over complete months before drawing conclusions about growth or decline. September may represent an incomplete reporting period.

## 3. Overall Business Recommendations

1. Improve onboarding completion through reminders and simpler registration steps.
2. Develop re-engagement campaigns for inactive users.
3. Continue monitoring Organic Search and Direct acquisition.
4. Evaluate regional user distribution to identify opportunities for expansion.
5. Optimize the web experience for Android and other commonly used devices.
6. Improve email verification completion.
7. Use permission-based marketing communication.
8. Review XP balances and consider engagement milestones.
9. Monitor monthly registration trends using complete reporting periods.

## 4. Analytical Limitations

This report is based only on the supplied Dataset 1 user profile table.

The dataset supports user profile, account status, onboarding status, acquisition, demographic, device, verification, consent, XP balance, activity-flag, and registration-date analysis.

It does not independently provide detailed session events, feature usage events, wallet transactions, withdrawal transactions, or referral conversion events. Therefore, these business activities cannot be measured from this dataset alone.

## 5. Conclusion

Dataset 1 provides useful insights into the user composition and account-related characteristics of VELoop Rewards. The analysis identifies opportunities to improve onboarding, user engagement, verification, acquisition monitoring, and the overall web experience.

These findings can support product and business decisions while keeping recommendations within the limits of the available data.
